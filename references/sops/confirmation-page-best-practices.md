# Confirmation Page Best Practices SOP
Source: Megalodon Marketing, presented by Jeremy Haynes

## 1. Introduction & Why Confirmation Pages Matter

The confirmation page is the first place a prospect lands after booking a call, opting into a webinar, or entering a challenge funnel. It directly influences show rate, frames the prospect's mindset, and shortens the sales cycle.

**Key reasons it matters:**
- Acknowledge the action: confirm they successfully booked or opted in.
- Address immediate objections: eliminate real reasons for them to bail.
- Accelerate due diligence: they're going to research you anyway, so guide them.

**Modern best practice:** skip the generic "Thanks, see you soon" page. Equip them with what they need to feel confident showing up. This is the gateway to a well-framed sales conversation.

## 2. Modern Due Diligence & Controlling the Narrative

Between booking and the actual call, prospects do their homework. If you don't guide that research, they'll find (or invent) answers on their own.

### 2.1 What people specifically look for
1. **Legitimacy & credibility** — Are these people real? Have they scammed anyone? Is their contact info/company presence legit? Who else has trusted them?
2. **Price & cost objections** — Why so expensive? Cheaper elsewhere? Worth the money? Financing available?
3. **Risk & worst-case scenarios** — What if this fails? What if they disappear or go bankrupt? Is the refund/guarantee real? Am I stuck paying if I don't see results?
4. **Timeline & logistics** — How long to see results? What's required of me? Will it conflict with my schedule? One-time or ongoing?
5. **Success & expectations** — Are testimonials cherry-picked? What's the realistic outcome? Can I see someone who didn't do well? How many people get big wins?
6. **Social proof & outside perspectives** — What does Reddit say? Are Trustpilot reviews genuine? Any social media rants? Recurring complaints?

### 2.2 Why you must steer the conversation
- Prospects crave transparency — unanswered questions get filled in with the worst assumption.
- Unaddressed skepticism becomes no-shows — one unexplained red flag and they vanish.
- You're their guide — people want to be led, even through the "bad" scenarios.

### 2.3 Common platforms & search behaviors
- **Reddit** — the #1 place for unfiltered opinions; people trust it more than official review sites.
- **Google** — "[Name] + Scam," "[Company] + Complaints," "[Offer] + Reviews" are standard searches.
- **YouTube & social media** — in-depth reviews, rants, exposés.

### 2.4 Best practices for controlling the narrative
1. **Acknowledge the downside** — if 1 in 10 doesn't succeed, say so and explain what you do in those cases. Honesty disarms skepticism fastest.
2. **Show them where to look** — point them to your Reddit threads/reviews with a mini-video on how to interpret what they'll find.
3. **Highlight third-party verification** — Trustpilot, BBB, known endorsers, low dispute rates (show the screenshot).
4. **Contextualize guarantees** — explain how and why the guarantee works, not just that it exists.
5. **Use breakout videos & testimonials** — organized answers signal you understand their concerns.

### 2.5 The outcome of guiding their research
- Higher show rates.
- Shorter sales cycles (less time proving legitimacy on the call).
- Better deal flow — by the time they talk to the closer, they're ~80% convinced.

## 3. The Main Confirmation Video

A short primary video at the top of the page that:
1. Welcomes & thanks them (briefly).
2. Explains you'll help them do due diligence.
3. Addresses major risks/objections head-on, including worst-case scenarios.
4. Redirects to further info on the page (breakout videos, testimonials).

**Pro tip line:** "I'm here to help you do your research so that by the time we speak, you know exactly whether this is for you."

**Tone:** transparent, no hype/fluff. Own any negative or doubtful points directly.

## 4. Breakout Videos: The Digestible Q&A Modules

Short (1–5 min) videos placed directly below the main video, each labeled by topic, tackling specific buyer questions.

### 4.1 Why breakout videos
- Digestible & on-demand vs. one long video.
- Stronger pre-call framing — kills repetitive Q&A that eats up half the sales call.
- Boosts trust — proactively addressing worst parts builds more trust, not less.

### 4.2 How to select topics
- Ask closers what objections eat up time or kill deals.
- Address downside/skepticism directly — don't avoid it.
- Mine public reviews/"scam" threads for recurring criticisms and make a video for each.

### 4.3 Crafting each mini-video
1. Title clearly (e.g. "What If It Doesn't Work?", "Why It Costs $XXXX", "Our Real Success Rate").
2. Open with the question directly.
3. Answer with evidence (dispute rate, refund policy screenshot, a real story).
4. Invite further review (watch more breakouts, ask on the call).
5. Keep it under 5 minutes.

### 4.4 Placement
- Cluster under the main video as "Common Questions" or "Important Topics Before Our Call."
- Order by priority — the most common objection goes first/most prominent.
- Mention the breakout section explicitly in the main video.

## 5. Testimonials & Trust Assets: Leveraging Real Transparency

Modern prospects want the full picture, not just highlight reels:
1. Home-run success stories.
2. Average/mid-level wins — more realistic, increases perceived honesty.
3. Worst-case or "less than ideal" outcomes — sharing these builds immense trust.

### 5.1 Third-party platforms
- Reddit — encourage real customers to post their experience.
- Video interviews — genuine, unscripted customer walkthroughs.

### 5.2 Quoting real data
- Dispute/refund rates as tangible proof, e.g. "0.7% dispute rate after 5,000 transactions."

## 6. Ongoing Optimization & Data Feedback

- **Listen to closers** — new objections popping up = new breakout video. Negative threads mentioned by leads = address proactively.
- **Track show rate & drop-off** — if under ~70% show rate, revisit confirmation content and breakout videos.
- **Refresh quarterly** — markets and rumors evolve; keep the page a living asset.

## 7. Synergizing Follow-Up (Emails, Texts, "Hammer Them" Content)

- **Value-dense emails** — go beyond reminders; link back to breakout videos and trust assets.
- **Short/long-form retargeting** — multiple pieces of content across FB/IG/YouTube in the days before the call, reiterating confirmation-page points.
- **Text & call reminders** — standard cadence: 24-hour, 2-hour, 10-minute reminders (possibly a final check call/text). Point back to the confirmation page if they claim not to have seen it.

## 8. Accelerating Your Sales Cycle & Final Action Steps

- **Show rate improvement:** transparent confirmation page + breakout videos + retargeting/email sequences can lift show rates from 50–60% to 80%+.
- **Stronger one-call closes:** fewer unanswered questions on the call → more "paid in full" commitments.
- **Continual updates:** watch for new public misconceptions and add content as needed.

### Immediate action items
1. Upgrade the confirmation page — updated trust assets, breakout videos, honest "real talk" about success and failure.
2. Sync follow-up — reminder emails/texts should direct back to the page or specific videos.
3. Monitor show rate & feedback — patch repeated objections into breakout videos ASAP.
