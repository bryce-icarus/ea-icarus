# Sales Script (2026)

## Phase 1 — Intro / Rapport
*Read the room. Match their energy. Some want small talk, some want to get straight to it.*

"Hey [Name], good to connect. Are you ready to dive in?"

## Phase 2 — Frame

"Cool. So as far as today goes, I'm more than happy to walk you through exactly what we do, how we do it, what results look like for brands in your space — all of that.

I don't want you to have to play a game of 21 questions, but if it's cool with you, I'd love to get some context on what you've got going on right now, what you've tried in the past, where you're looking to take things — just to make sure there's synergy on both sides. And then from there, assuming it makes sense, I'll walk you through everything on our end.

Is it cool if we run things that way?"

## Phase 3 — Situation (5 min)
*Get the facts here.*

- "Walk me through the brand — what are you selling, what's the hero SKU, what's a typical order value?"
- "Where's revenue coming from right now — Meta, Google, email, affiliates, influencers?"
- "What are you spending on Meta a day right now, and what's ROAS looking like?"
- "Who's running it — in-house, a freelancer, another agency?"
- "What's your account situation? Own BM, rented, agency accounts, been banned before?"
- "How many times have you been shut down in the last 12 months?"

## Phase 3 — Discovery

**Current Situation**
"Walk me through what you've got going on right now on the marketing and traffic side — how are you currently bringing customers in?"

*(Listen. Then get numbers — never "how's that going for you.")*

"Fill me in on the numbers — what does monthly traffic or customer acquisition actually look like?"

### Path A — Push Away (numbers sound decent)
"Honestly man, between you and I — those are actually pretty solid. Is there any reason you couldn't just double down on what you're already doing? In theory you would double the business."

*(Shut up. Let them justify why they need something else.)*

### Path B — Poke Holes (clear constraint visible)
"Got it. Sounds like you're getting traffic but it's just a consistency thing — or maybe more of an infrastructure constraint. Am I reading that right?"

*(Never say "problem." Use: thing, constraint, bottleneck, challenge.)*

### Circle the Constraint
"So it sounds like the core thing really is **[their constraint in their words]**. Is that the main thing, or is there something more underneath it?"

### Timeline
"How long has that been a constraint for you?"

*(Store this for Why Now.)*

### Impact
"Super salesy question here — but how do you think [constraint] has actually been impacting things? Revenue, ability to scale, time you're burning — how do you feel it's been playing out?"

*(Slow down. Let them feel it.)*

### Cost of Inaction
"I'm sure the business isn't going anywhere — but if this just stays where it is another 6–12 months, what does that actually cost you? Not just in revenue, but in growth you're not capturing?"

### Why Now
"You mentioned you've been dealing with this for [X time]. What makes now the time to actually get something different in place?"

### Goals *(only if they're open — skip if closed off)*
"If we actually solved [constraint] — is there a specific revenue target or growth goal you're working toward in the next 6–12 months?"

### Reality Check
"Just being straight with you — with what you've got in place right now, do you think you can realistically get there as quickly as you'd like? Or does something need to change?"

## Phase 4 — Transition to Pitch

"That all makes a lot of sense to me. So the way I'm hearing it — the core thing is [constraint in their words], you've been dealing with it for [X time], and you're trying to get to [goal]. Am I off on anything?"

"Perfect. I'm ready to go through things on my side — but anything else about where you're at that'd be useful for me to know?"

*(Listen. They may hand you the close right here.)*

## Phase 5 — Pitch

### The Grand Idea
"The whole thing is built around one idea: right now you're selling to the 10% who already know what they want. Everything we build is designed to reach the other 90%. Here's how that gets done."

### Week 1 — Infrastructure (30 sec)
"First thing we do is move you off your accounts entirely. You run on our Tier 1 accounts — direct Meta partnership, Gold and Platinum rated. Not your BM, not a rented one, ours.

That's what lets us run product-forward creative instead of the sanitized version, and it's why our campaigns sit live for 180+ days. Takes 7-10 business days."

### Week 1-2 — Research (90 sec — the money)
"While that's building, we're doing the research, and this is the part most agencies skip entirely. We go into the forums, the Reddit threads, the reviews, and we come back with your actual buyers — usually four or five distinct people.

For a peptide brand it's almost always something like: the guy who's dropped six hundred bucks on bunk product and now won't touch anything without a Janoshik COA. The 42-year-old whose recovery fell apart and has never heard the word peptide in his life. The biohacker who wants mechanism, not testimonials.

Three completely different people. Different pain, different vocabulary, different reason to buy. Right now they're all seeing the same ad and all landing on the same page — which is why only one of them converts.

So each one gets their own ad set, their own creative angle, and their own landing page. The skeptic gets bloodwork and lab results. The 42-year-old gets an educational hook that doesn't mention the product for ten seconds. That's how you get to the 90%.

It's also, specifically, what Meta's algorithm rewards now — it needs creative variety to match micro-segments, and most brands starve it with four ads on one angle. We feed it. That's the gap between 2-3% conversion and 5-8%."

**Check in here. Only here.**
"Make sense so far?"

### Week 2-3 — Live (40 sec)
"By day 14 to 21 everything's live — all personas, all pages, retargeting running. From there it's weekly creative production. Static, video, real UGC, AI UGC. Every week, not one batch a month.

Weeks 1-3 we're finding out which personas and which pages win. Weeks 4-8 we scale those and kill the rest."

### Month 3+ — Compounding (25 sec)
"Month three is where budget really starts moving, because now we're scaling proven winners instead of guessing. Attribution's clean the whole way — Triple Whale and Hyros, click-based, not inflated view-through numbers. You get weekly reporting and you'll always know what's actually driving revenue."

### Bridge to Price
"So that's the build. Before I give you the number — you said the goal is [their number] by [timeline], and the thing in the way is [their words]. That's still it, right?"

### Price Drop
"Tier 1 infrastructure, full persona research, the landing pages, weekly creative, campaign management and reporting — it's $3,500 a month plus ad spend. We'd want you at $150 to $200 a day to start.

Month to month. No contract. And if the ads stop running or the account goes down, you don't pay the retainer that month."

### Commitment
"One thing I want to be straight with you on, because month-to-month cuts both ways. No long term contract means you can leave whenever. It also means some brands come in thinking they'll test it for thirty days — and thirty days is genuinely not enough to know anything. Weeks 1 to 3 we're testing, so month one tells you which personas are working, not what the machine does.

You'll know by day 45 whether this is working. Month three is where it actually compounds. So I'm not asking you to sign anything — but I am asking you to come in planning on ninety days, not thirty. Is that where your head's at?

So based on everything we've covered — does this feel like the right move to get you to [their number]?"
