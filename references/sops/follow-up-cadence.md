# Cadence That Converts

## Speed-to-Lead (Day 1)
- Call within 2–5 minutes of the lead coming in.
- No pickup? Call again in 60 min, then again at 3 hours.
- SMS: "Yo [Name] — saw you checked out [Offer]. 10 mins later today or tomorrow to see if it's a fit?"

## Reactivation (14-day sequence)
- **Day 1:** Call + SMS (pattern interrupt)
- **Day 2:** Email — case study tied to their industry
- **Day 3:** Call + voicemail (value, not vagueness)
- **Day 4:** SMS with 2 slots ("2:15 or 3:40 better?")
- **Day 6:** Call with new angle (different problem)
- **Day 8:** Email + Loom (90 seconds, screen proof)
- **Day 10:** Call — "last slot this week" (truthful)
- **Day 12:** SMS: "Still relevant? Yes/No"
- **Day 14:** Final email — close the loop, re-open later

## Dialing Principles (non-negotiables)
- Local presence dialing is a trap — doesn't lift pickup rate and risks compliance issues. Don't do it.
- Roster correctly: speed-to-lead wins. Right rep, right window, right follow-up.
- After 10 dials on the same lead, pickup probability drops under 3% and success rate ~0.05%. Stop burning time — switch channels or move to the next sequence step.

## Activity Targets (quality over vanity)
- Dials: 150/day, 750/week
- Live conversations: 12/day, 60/week
- Appointments: 3/day, 15/week
- Show rate: 70%+
- Close rate: 20–30%
- Follow-ups scheduled: 5/day, 25/week
- Revenue: set monthly target, reverse-engineer daily/weekly activity from it

## Weekly Rhythm
- **Monday:** Set pipeline targets, schedule roleplay
- **Tue–Thu:** Execute full cadence, prioritize fresh leads + hot leads + reactivations
- **Friday:** Pipeline audit, update objection log, capture lessons learned
- **Weekend (optional):** 30 min script reps
