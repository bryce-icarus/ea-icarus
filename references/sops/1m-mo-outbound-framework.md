# $1M/mo Outbound Framework

Placeholders used throughout: `(NAME)` = your name, `(CREATOR)` = the brand/personality behind the offer, `(OFFER)` = the specific offer/niche, `(GOAL)` = the prospect's stated goal, `(CLOSER NAME)` = the closer being booked.

**Items marked MANDATORY must be asked to set a lead.** Probe on any small detail the prospect gives you between questions.

## Intro
- "Hey ___?"
- "Hey this is (NAME), with (CREATOR) (OFFER)'s Team. I saw you filled out our form and got our free course to get help with (OFFER), is that right?"
- **Set frame:** "Yeah I'm just reaching out real quick to see IF we can even help you out with (OFFER) and if I feel like we can and there is alignment I'll explain how we can do that step-by-step. Sounds good?"
- "How did you find out about (CREATOR)?" *(ask if they seem closed off)*

## Goals / Why
More probing here = easier pitch later.

- "Are you looking to get into (OFFER) or are you already doing (OFFER) at the moment?"
- "What got you interested in (OFFER)?" *(if 1 year or less in)*
- "What's the ultimate goal here with (OFFER)?" *(if 1+ years in)*
- "What are you looking to gain from the free course?"
- "What's making you feel like that though?"
- "How long have you been trying to get to (GOAL)?"
- "Why aren't we there yet? / What have you seen so far that's making you feel you aren't there yet?"
- "In order for you to reach (GOAL), how much would you have to be making on a monthly basis?"
- "What else would $Xk a month allow you to do, other than [goals already stated]?"

### Financial qualification (MANDATORY)
- "In order for you to achieve (GOAL) so you can XYZ, how much capital do we have set aside to invest into the opportunity to learn and increase your (OFFER) ability to make that change?"
- "Is that for (OFFER) specifically, or can you use that to invest in yourself to get the proper help with (OFFER)? And I'm not asking for that now."
- **Double down (MANDATORY):** "Sometimes when I ask people that question they say 'yeah I have it but they need time to come up with it or liquidate it' — is that the case with you, or do you already have it at the moment? Again, I'm not asking for that now."
  - (If yes) "Ok yeah, that sounds like a doable budget to get you help with everything."

### Time
- "How much time do we have set aside to put towards (OFFER) every week?"

*Go back into pain if it makes sense — reference "Deeper Problem/Goals Awareness Sequence Part 2" as a guide.*

## Recommendation / Set

**Can I share a recommendation?**
- (Negative) "If you are seriously trying to get (GOAL), this is great but it's not going to get you all the way there, especially with you struggling with [their stated problems]."
- (Positive) "What I will say, if you really want to get (GOAL), what's really going to help you out is for you to get guidance where you could work with (CREATOR) on getting you to (GOAL) — does that sound like something that's in alignment with what you're looking for?"

**Set the call:**
- "So why don't we do this. For anyone looking into getting more help with (OFFER) we usually schedule a bit of a longer meeting with (CREATOR)'s right hand man/woman, (CLOSER NAME). They're well versed in (OFFER) just like (CREATOR), and I can see them being able to look into your situation, see how we can get you to (GOAL), and if there is alignment we'll show you how we can help — so with that said, what time tomorrow works best for you to take a 1 hour call on Zoom?"
- Double-confirm the time aloud; ask if they've checked for conflicts.
- "Other than an earthquake or a tsunami, will there be any reason why you may miss this call?" / "Are you sure? Haha"

## Authority (decision-maker check)
- "Did you have a biz partner or a spouse involved when it comes to making more money as well, or just you?"
  - **If yes:** "Awesome, why don't you bring them on the call so they can ask questions and understand everything with you."
  - **If no:** "Ok are you sure? Reason I'm asking is because sometimes people have joint accounts with their spouse or business partner and use that in order to (OFFER)."
  - **If pushback ("I need to run it by them"):** "Is it more of a you-need-permission-from-them type of thing, or you just want to let them know? Because sometimes people say they need permission from their spouse in order to use the money to (OFFER)."
    - If permission is genuinely needed, make sure the spouse/partner is on the call.

- "What would you say are the main 1 or 2 questions you'd want to ask (CLOSER NAME) on the call?"
- "Make sure they aren't driving": "Also the call is gonna be about 45 min – 1 hour on Zoom. Will you be in a quiet spot, ready to take notes, in front of a computer or tablet with time set aside?" *(phone is fine as long as they're not driving)*

## Confirm Calendar
"So I'm sending you a calendar invite. Just do me a favor while I have you on the phone — check to see if you got the invite and press accept. We have an assistant that works for us, and she is brutal — anything that is not accepted, she just automatically erases it off the calendar… do you see the invitation?"

## Pre-Call Video (MANDATORY homework)
"Also, I do have some homework here for ya. It's nothing too crazy but it is mandatory from (CREATOR) — it's a link with a short video of (CREATOR) going over the call and what to expect, plus a bunch of testimonials from a lot of our successful students. So if I were to send this over, how soon could you knock this out before your call on (said date)?"

Send the pre-call video link while still on the call.

Close: "Have a good day, we'll see you on (said date) @ (time of booked call)"
