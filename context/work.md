## Sales & Delivery Process (observed from real email threads)
1. Lead books a "Strategy Session with Icarus - 30min" via Calendly.
2. Simon Jordan sends the calendar invite and assigns a ClickUp task under Sales/CRM/Leads; a "Discovery Call Prep Assistant" auto-generates a pre-call brief in ClickUp (frequently flags low-confidence CRM matches — thin lead records with no website/notes are common, worth enriching before the call if time allows).
3. Post-call, Bryce sends a "Stage 1 paid-media plan" proposal — hosted at `help.icarusdigitalmarketing.com/assets/[client-name]-proposal.html`. **Takes a while to build, not same-day.** Structure (per the Caleb Dornay/Nic Nac example, referenced for shape/understanding only, never copied as content): title/header → "What you asked us to do" (restates the client's stated goals) → "The objective" (acquisition targets, ROAS threshold, channel focus) → audience/data constraints (e.g. new-customers-only, exclusion rules) → "The budget & ramp" (spend/timeline parameters), likely continuing into strategy/pricing/next-steps not fully visible via fetch. Day-1 follow-up copy should never assume this has already gone out, check Bryce's sent mail for actual confirmation first (see `lead-follow-up` and `day1-followup-auto`), and default to mentioning it's coming rather than already sent.
4. Contract goes out via **SignWell** ("Icarus Digital Marketing - Meta" doc) — view/complete events trigger email notifications per signer.
5. Once signed, Finance invoices; once paid, onboarding kicks off in ClickUp under `Delivery / [Client] -- Retainer / 1. Client Onboarding`, tasks assigned by Simon Jordan.
6. Meetings are auto-transcribed/summarized via **Gemini notes** or **read.ai**.
